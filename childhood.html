Childhood — How It All Started

Tech got me hooked early. My dad showed me computers and how they tick—from the hardware to the way systems run. That curiosity stuck.

Movies about hacking and tech didn’t just entertain me—they made me wonder what’s really going on behind the scenes. But the real spark came when I saw my older brother bypass passwords. That was wild. I realized that with the right knowledge, you can understand and control systems. That moment pushed me to dive deeper into software.

Still learning. Still building.
